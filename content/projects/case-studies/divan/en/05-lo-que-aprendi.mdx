- **Listening to the person who uses it beats guessing.** One afternoon going through the app with the psychologist changed columns, wording and forms I thought were finished.
- **Removing is designing too.** After each stage I went screen by screen looking for what was left over. Every step that disappeared made the app easier to understand.
- **Words matter.** “Gap” was handy for me, but on screen it reads “free slot”, the way the patient sees it. The wording was reviewed so it makes sense without knowing the app.
- **An action shouldn’t require earlier steps.** If booking needs the patient to exist first, the app handles it in the same form.
- **Plan for what comes after, too.** Data backups and the free plan’s limits were worked out before inviting anyone, not when they became necessary.
