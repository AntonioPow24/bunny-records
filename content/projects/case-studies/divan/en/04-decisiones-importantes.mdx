- **Organise, don’t interpret.** Diván shows what the psychologist writes and nothing more: there are no diagnosis fields or automatic conclusions, and it never compares patients. It’s an organising tool, not a clinical record.
- **Nothing is deleted on its own.** A patient who finishes is archived and their information stays. Changes to appointments, payments and notes are recorded.
- **The app warns, it doesn’t impose.** Dates are agreed on the go: if an appointment falls outside working hours, the app says so and still lets you save it. A legitimate exception shouldn’t need a workaround.
- **WhatsApp without integrations.** Instead of connecting to WhatsApp, the app opens the message already written. It’s simpler, costs nothing and the psychologist decides what gets sent.
- **Premium means more information, not more settings.** What Premium adds are features that save work, like next-day reminders or attendance certificates as PDF, not a panel full of options.
