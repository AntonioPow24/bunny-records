Diván brings the practice together in one place and opens like an app from the phone’s or computer’s home screen, with no app store.

- **Today:** the day’s appointments, the next session with what was left from the previous one, what’s due to be collected and the tasks coming due.
- **Schedule:** the week with free slots in view. Picking a date means tapping a slot, with no overlaps, and free slots can be shared on WhatsApp as a message and an image.
- **Patients:** a practical record with contact details, appointments, notes, tasks and payments.
- **Close session:** when a session ends, a single screen marks attendance, saves a short summary and suggests the next appointment.
- **Payments:** per session or per package, by Yape, Plin, bank transfer or cash, with the month’s takings.

WhatsApp is still the channel: the app prepares the message and the psychologist just taps send. And the data is theirs: they can take it to a spreadsheet whenever they want.
