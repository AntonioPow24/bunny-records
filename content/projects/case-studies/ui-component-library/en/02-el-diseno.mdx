I started from the Bunny Records brand: its four colours (Midnight, Teal, Ivory and Ink) and its two typefaces. From there I set myself a simple rule: **no component decides its own colour, spacing or pace**. Everything comes from a shared base, so changing the look of a whole project takes minutes.

- **Three looks:** dark, light and a high-contrast one for those who need it, all checked to read well.
- **Design and code speak the same language:** what you see in Figma and what reaches the screen come from the same source, so they never drift apart.
- **Built for real cases, not just the ideal one:** every piece covers what happens while loading, when there’s nothing to show or when something fails, and works the same with a mouse, a keyboard or a finger.

The catalogue has its own logo: a bunny made of three interface pieces, two ears and a base, as a nod to the label.
