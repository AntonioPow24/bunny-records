Before writing a single line of code I designed everything in Figma: colours, typefaces, components and screens for desktop and mobile. The site follows that design to the letter.

The bunny was modelled in 3D in Blender as a vinyl figure and exported in parts (head, ears, eyes, arms) and in several poses. On the web those parts are animated separately: it blinks, breathes, follows the pointer and dances. It looks 3D, but it’s really images, so the page stays light.

The decision that changed the design most came after the first version: **on desktop, no screen scrolls**. Everything fits in the window and long content scrolls inside its panel. About and Contact became discs in the bar that drop onto a turntable when pressed. On mobile, those two pages do scroll as a whole when they don’t fit.
