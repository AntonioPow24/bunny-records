- **Measure before optimising.** The transition between discs felt slow. Measuring it showed the connection wasn’t to blame: the new screen’s bunny was getting ready right in the middle of the animation. Delaying its arrival, loading only the pose it needed and shortening the movement made it clearly smoother.
- **Test the real version.** While developing, everything runs slower and shows warnings that don’t exist later; measurements only count on the published version.
- **Don’t trust a single tool.** An automated test said the page was slow to load; tried under real conditions, it loaded much faster.
- **Even fonts matter.** While the typeface loaded, the text jumped slightly on mobile. Choosing the right stand-in font got rid of it.
- **AI as help, the decisions as mine.** One tool helped me propose and review the design, another helped build it. I approved each design before it went into code, and that avoided duplicated work.
