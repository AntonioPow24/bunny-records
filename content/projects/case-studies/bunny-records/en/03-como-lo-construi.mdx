- **Fast by design.** Every page is prepared in advance, so it opens instantly and doesn’t depend on any server.
- **Two languages from day one.** The whole site is in Spanish and English, and no text can be published if it’s missing in either.
- **Content that looks after itself.** Projects, tracks and credits follow fixed rules: for example, a project that doesn’t exist yet can’t show a demo or results.
- **Seamless navigation.** Nothing reloads when you change screens: the cover travels to the album header and the bar’s disc drops onto the turntable.
- **Every technology has its own sheet**, which opens as a panel without leaving About, or as its own page when visited directly.
- **Quality checked** on the published version: that it works with a keyboard, is accessible, loads quickly on mobile and is secure.
