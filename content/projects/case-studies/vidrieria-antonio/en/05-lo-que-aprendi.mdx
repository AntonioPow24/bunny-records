- **Test against the production build.** In development everything worked. In production, Next cached the queries for a year and the site didn’t reflect changes from the panel. I fixed it by revalidating the public site on every write, with a one-hour expiry as a safety net.
- **Content is the bottleneck.** Without real photos the design can’t be validated; ask for them before building the layout.
- **A proposal is won with a trial.** Offering a trial period on a temporary, non-indexed address lowered the risk for both sides.
- **Visual feedback comes early.** The first palette didn’t represent the business; changing it in time was cheaper than defending it.
