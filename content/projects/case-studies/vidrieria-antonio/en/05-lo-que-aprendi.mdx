- **Test the real version, not just the working one.** While developing, everything worked, but on the published version changes from the panel took a long time to show up on the site. I fixed it by making the site update every time something is saved.
- **Content is the bottleneck.** Without real photos the design can’t be validated; ask for them before building the layout.
- **A proposal is won with a trial.** Offering a trial period on a temporary address, hidden from search engines, lowered the risk for both sides.
- **Feedback on the look comes early.** The first palette didn’t represent the business; changing it in time was cheaper than defending it.
