- **A site that’s fast and easy to maintain.** Pages show what’s stored directly, with no extra pieces in between that could break.
- **Security where it really matters.** Anyone can view the site, but only the business can change it, and that rule lives in the database itself, not just on screen.
- **A panel that helps avoid mistakes.** Forms point out straight away if something is missing or invalid, before anything is saved.
- **Photos that prepare themselves.** They’re shrunk on the phone or computer before uploading, so they load quickly and nobody needs to know about formats.
- **Colours chosen to read well**, checking that every pairing has enough contrast.
- **A small automatic daily task** that keeps the free service holding the data from going to sleep through lack of use.
