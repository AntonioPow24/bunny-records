- **WhatsApp instead of a form.** I removed the quote form, the table that stored it and the email service: they didn’t fit how the customers communicate.
- **No made-up testimonials.** The section was dropped until there are real ones.
- **No external CMS.** Supabase already covered data, login and files; a CMS would have duplicated the data layer.
- **Hosting that respects the terms.** Vercel’s free plan is for non-commercial use only, so the trial runs on Netlify.
- **Stable URLs.** Editing a project’s title no longer changes its address on its own, so shared links don’t break.
- **A generic brand during the trial.** The site uses a neutral name and placeholders until the business decides.
