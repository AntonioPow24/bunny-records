A company website with its own admin panel:

- **Projects as social proof.** Not a decorative gallery: they can be filtered by category so each audience quickly reaches what matters to them, with detail pages and a gallery.
- **Services** with their own page and the real projects of that category at the end.
- **Direct contact over WhatsApp** with a pre-written message. The business’s customers use WhatsApp, not email, so a form would have been friction.
- **Admin panel** to create and edit projects, services, copy and contact details, with photo uploads.

All with its own identity (blue and red), which came out of the client’s feedback on a first version.
