A company website with its own admin panel:

- **Projects as a calling card.** Not a decorative gallery: they’re filtered by category so each audience quickly reaches what matters to them, with detail pages and galleries.
- **Services**, each with its own page and the real projects in that category at the end.
- **Direct contact via WhatsApp** with a pre-written message. The business’s customers use WhatsApp, not email, so a form would have been an obstacle.
- **An admin panel** to create and edit projects, services, text and contact details, with photo uploads.

All with its own identity (blue and red) that came from the client’s feedback on a first version.
